About Me

Hi, I'm Princess.

I'm Princess Mhlongo, a software developer, technology enthusiast, and Computer Science student based in Durban, South Africa.

I believe technology is at its best when it solves real problems and creates meaningful opportunities. Whether I'm developing software, building websites, exploring artificial intelligence, or learning about cybersecurity, my goal is always the same — to create solutions that improve lives, empower businesses, and make technology more accessible.

My interests span software engineering, full-stack web development, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and digital innovation. I enjoy transforming ideas into practical, scalable solutions that combine thoughtful design with reliable engineering.

Alongside my studies in Computer and Information Sciences (Application Development) at Emeris University, I've built applications, collaborated on technology initiatives, participated in hackathons, attended industry events, and contributed to youth development and entrepreneurship programmes through Tech Horizons. These experiences have strengthened not only my technical skills but also my ability to lead, communicate, solve problems, and work effectively with diverse teams.

Beyond coding, I'm passionate about continuous learning and knowledge sharing. I enjoy mentoring aspiring developers, connecting with professionals across the tech industry, and exploring how emerging technologies can help young people, entrepreneurs, and underserved communities unlock new opportunities.

Every project I take on is an opportunity to learn, grow, and create something that delivers real value. I approach my work with curiosity, attention to detail, and a commitment to building technology that makes a lasting impact.

This is only the beginning — and I'm excited for what's next.

"Building technology that empowers people, businesses, and communities."
